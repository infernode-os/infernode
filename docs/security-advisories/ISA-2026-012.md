## Summary

`lib/voice/listen` (and `listen-opus`) announced a 9P export on a TCP
port (7070 by default) so a peer could mount this machine's `/dev` and
carry audio over it. The export was anonymous and was all of `/dev`,
not only the audio files.

## Impact

While a voice listener ran, anyone who could reach its port could mount
`/dev` with no credentials: inject input into the user's session,
operate the control devices bound there, and record from the
microphone.

## Fix

The listener builds a fresh export holding only `audio` and
`audioctl`, and listeners and dialers authenticate with an explicit
keyring. There is no anonymous mode in the shipped voice scripts; a
node needs a signer and authentication information
(`auth/createsignerkey`, `auth/mkauthinfo`) before voice works.

## Workarounds

Do not run `lib/voice/listen` or `listen-opus` on a release before
0.10.0; if you did, treat any machine that could reach its port as
having had control of the session. Update to 0.10.0 or later.
