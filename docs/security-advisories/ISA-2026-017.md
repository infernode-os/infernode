## Summary

devssl, which protects every connection InferNode encrypts (mount,
listen, cpu, login, secstore, the registries), offered `sha256` (SSL 2's
hash of secret and data, not an HMAC) and AES-CBC with padded records,
and took the same secret for both directions, so a record could be sent
back to its sender and accepted.

## Impact

Someone on the path could reflect a peer's own records back to it, and
the hash-of-secret MAC and padded CBC records were weaker than their
names suggested.

## Fix

ssl(3) offers `aes_256_gcm` (the header authenticated, key and nonce by
HKDF-SHA-384, the record number in the nonce, so a record changed, cut,
replayed, reordered or reflected is refused) and `hmac_sha384` for
integrity alone. Each direction has its own secret of at least 32
bytes. Both ends must run 0.10.1 or later.

## Workarounds

None. Update both ends to 0.10.1 or later.

Found internally.
