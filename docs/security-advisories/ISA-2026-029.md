## Summary

In devssl (hosted and native):

- under `hmac_sha384`, a record of no bytes (its two-byte header
  arriving alone) was dereferenced through nil before any MAC was
  checked;
- the data paths took the conversation number from the channel's qid
  without asking which file it was, so a conversation given another
  conversation's directory as its descriptor (anyone may open one) read
  and wrote that conversation's plaintext;
- a second descriptor replaced the channel under readers and writers,
  and the conversation table was grown by `realloc` under readers that
  held no lock.

## Impact

Anyone who could send bytes to an `hmac_sha384` connection could end
the emulator (or panic a native board) before authentication. A local
program, a confined one included, could read and write the decrypted
data of another program's encrypted connection.

## Fix

A record shorter than its MAC is refused first; only a conversation's
data file is served, and only a channel open both ways that is not a
directory, a mount or one of ssl's own files may be pushed; the table
is allocated whole.

## Workarounds

None. Update to 0.11.0 or later.

Found by the project's security review.
