## Summary

wikifs, served by `httpd -w`, lets anyone who reaches it write a page.
A page's title, heading, author and comment, the edit form's `value`
and `<textarea>`, and a link's URL were written into the HTML as they
were: `</textarea><script>` ran when anyone pressed Edit, a link could
be `javascript:` or break out of its `href`.

## Impact

Anyone who could edit a wiki could run script in the browser of anyone
who viewed or edited the page.

## Fix

Each field is escaped; a link is to a page or an http, https, ftp,
mailto, telnet or file URL, or is shown as text.

## Workarounds

On a release before 0.11.0, do not serve a wiki with `httpd -w` to
people who may write it.

Found by the project's own testing.
