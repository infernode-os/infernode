gps - Where this machine is, from its GPS receiver

Reads the GPS receiver at /mnt/gps (gpsfs): the position, the whole
fix, the receiver's state, the satellites in view, or waits for the
next fix. Read only: it cannot choose the receiver or set a position.
A location is personal: use it for what the user asked, and do not
send it anywhere they did not ask you to.

Usage:
  gps [position]       latitude and longitude, decimal degrees (north, east positive)
  gps fix              the whole fix, attr=value: time lat lon alt (metres above sea
                       level) speed (m/s) course (degrees true) mode q sats hdop err (metres)
  gps status           state=fix|nofix|nosource, the source, the age of the fix
  gps satellites       one line a satellite: system prn elevation azimuth snr used
  gps wait [seconds]   wait for the next fix (default 30, at most 300)

"error: no fix" means the receiver has no position now; gps status says
why. "error: no GPS receiver" means /mnt/gps is not served.

Examples:
  gps
  gps fix
  gps wait 10
