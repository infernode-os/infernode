hear - What the user says next (speech-to-text)

Usage:
  hear                       Wait up to 30 s for the user to speak
  hear <ms>                  Wait up to ms (at most 120000)

The user answers by pressing the microphone key and speaking; you get
the words of what they say. Ask them first (say), since nothing is
heard unless they choose to answer.

Requires speech(4) at /mnt/speech.
