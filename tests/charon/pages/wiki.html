Inferno (operating system)

Inferno
DeveloperBell Labs, Vita Nuova Holdings
Written inC, Limbo
OS familyPlan 9
Initial release1996
LicenseMIT

Inferno is a distributed operating system started at Bell Labs and now developed and maintained by Vita Nuova Holdings as free software under the MIT License. Inferno was based on the experience gained with Plan 9 from Bell Labs, and the further research of Bell Labs into operating systems, languages, on-the-fly compilers, graphics, security, networking and portability.

Applications are written in the type-safe language Limbo, whose binary representation is identical over all platforms, and are executed using a just-in-time compilation technique in the Dis virtual machine.
